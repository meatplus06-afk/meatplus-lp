# W2 storefront search discovery

`w2-sitemap.xml` lists the W2 shop homepage and the parent purchase URL for
every published guide product. It is regenerated by
`scripts/build-commerce-discovery.mjs` during normal publication. It does not
claim to list all products sold by W2.

Both `https://meat-plus.club/` and the GitHub Pages guide prefix must have
verified ownership in the same Search Console account. Google supports
cross-site sitemap submission for verified properties:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap#cross-submit

Submit `w2-sitemap.xml` from the verified GitHub Pages guide property's
Sitemaps report. Its URLs belong to the separately verified W2 shop.
The shop property's URL-prefix form rejects an external full URL, so use
the property hosting the file. The guide's own `sitemap.xml` remains separate.
Submission acceptance is not proof of processing or indexing.
Do not submit a redirect route, UTM tracking URL, or individual SKU URL when
the shop uses the parent product as its canonical page.

No last-modified date is supplied: guide publication dates do not establish
when the shop page changed. No product brand is inferred from the seller's
name, a category, meat origin, or a product title.

On 2026-10-07 the guide sitemap passed Google's live inspection (fetch
successful, crawling allowed); its sitemap report still showed a fetch
failure. Do not equate live fetch success with sitemap processing or
indexing completion. The shop property was verified through existing
Google Analytics and its homepage was already indexed. New property reports
initially require Google processing.

The 52 public shop product pages checked on that date supplied no JSON-LD
product brand. The 70-SKU commerce preparation remains incomplete until
the real brands shown on the shop pages are verified. OpenAI feed access
also requires partner approval; an application receipt does not establish
feed access or ingestion.
